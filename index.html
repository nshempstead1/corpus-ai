Process

How it works

  1. Pay $149. Stripe checkout. On the success page, send the suspension email, public profile URL, and the documents.
  2. We extract and check. Name and address on every document must match the profile, including suite line. Utility bill must be dated within 90 days. At least three documents from Google’s accepted list.
  3. A person releases the packet. Mismatches are a hold, not a ship. You get the folder and the letter, or a gap report.
  4. You upload in one sitting. Follow the run sheet. Do not add a Fiverr manager to the listing — Local Search Forum reports those accounts can cause another suspension.

FAQ

Questions operators ask

Do you log into my Google account?

No. You submit from your own account. We never ask for your password or manager access.

Do you guarantee Google will reinstate my listing?

No. We guarantee a complete, policy-mapped packet within 4 business hours of complete intake. Google’s decision is Google’s. Appealing a profile that still violates the rules can make the next review harder.

How long do I have to upload evidence?

Google’s help documentation (support.google.com/business/answer/4569145) states that once the evidence form opens, you have 60 minutes to attach files or they may not go through.

What if my documents do not match?

We do not fabricate documents. You get a gap report of what is missing, or a refund.

No. Corpus AI is a DBA of Corpus Localization LLC. This product is not a certified translation. Reviews for this packet should not be left on the translation listing.
